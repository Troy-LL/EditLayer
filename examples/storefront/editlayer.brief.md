# Fieldnote design brief

**Voice:** Calm, premium, honest. No hype—describe materials and use, not lifestyle fantasy.

**Typography:** Tight headings (negative letter-spacing on titles). Body copy at generous line height (~1.65). System sans stack; weight 600 for titles, 500 for UI.

**Color:** Ink `#1f2421` on warm off-white `#f7f5f0`. One accent: deep sage `#3d5a4c` for CTAs and highlights. Muted text `#5c6560` for secondary copy.

**Spacing:** 8px base scale. Sections feel airy (`4.5rem`+ vertical rhythm). Cards breathe with `1.5rem` internal padding.

**Components:** Buttons are pill-shaped (`border-radius: 999px`). Product cards use hairline borders, not drop shadows. Hover states are subtle border or color shifts only.

**Motion:** Minimal. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No decorative animation on load.

**Imagery:** Product “photos” are color swatches—gradients from the material tone. Keep them calm, not neon.
